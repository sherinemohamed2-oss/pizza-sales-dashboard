## 📊 Dashboard Visuals & Key Metrics Included

1. **Total Orders by Pizza Size**
   * Visualizes order distribution across sizes (**L**, **M**, **S**, **XL**) using a breakdown chart.
2. **Top 5 Peak Hours by Orders**
   * Highlights rush hours (**5 PM**, **1 PM**, **6 PM**, **12 PM**, and **7 PM**) to assist with staff scheduling and kitchen efficiency.
3. **Sales Percentage by Category**
   * Displays revenue proportion across menu categories (**Veggie**, **Classic**, **Supreme**, and **Chicken**).
4. **Top 10 Pizzas by Quantity**
   * Ranks the best-selling menu items (such as *The Classic Deluxe Pizza*, *The Pepperoni Pizza*, and *The Four Cheese Pizza*) by units sold.
5. **Order Trend by Month**
   * Tracks monthly order fluctuations and seasonal demand trends throughout the year (from January to December).
